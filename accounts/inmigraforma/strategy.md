# Strategy — @inmigraforma

Repositioned 2026-07 from promoting immigration services to **US immigration
news, explained bilingually (Spanish-first)**. The account has a small real
audience (~162 followers) acquired under the old positioning — treat them as
Spanish-speaking people personally affected by US immigration policy.

## What the performance brief actually supports (2026-08-06)

The brief for this batch says: **not yet meaningful — total signal 93 across 12
posts, and 150 is the threshold to rank posts at all**. That is less usable
information than the previous snapshot, not more. Nothing in it distinguishes
one post, format or topic from another, and it still reports no saves, shares,
reach or profile visits — the metrics this account was repositioned around.

Everything written in the 2026-07-30 assessment therefore still stands:

- The historic top and bottom performers are **pre-repositioning posts** whose
  hook, slide count and tags are recorded as "?" — there is nothing concrete to
  copy from them.
- The only identified post-repositioning item ever measured (post01) was
  measured roughly one day after publication. That is not evidence about method
  posts, prompt slides or the asylum topic.
- With 93 total engagement below the ranking threshold, **no post-level
  comparison is legitimate**. Not "the news posts beat the method posts", not
  the reverse.

So: no hypothesis moves this batch either. Everything is carried forward
unchanged, with the missing evidence named. Batch 3 changes format only where
there is an internal, craft-level reason.

## Non-negotiables (not hypotheses)

- News comes ONLY from official government outlets (USCIS, DHS, State
  Department, Federal Register, EOIR). No secondary-press reporting as news.
- Immigration-lawyer commentary is allowed ONLY as clearly labelled opinion
  ("OPINIÓN", lawyer/firm named) with the not-legal-advice disclaimer, never on
  a cover.
- Every claim carries a source and a date on the slide itself.
- No individual legal advice, ever. The disclaimer line closes every caption.
- Never alarmist. People make life decisions on this information.
- Visual continuity: keep the account's existing style — no big changes in
  colors or fonts (owner's instruction, 2026-07-27).
- No caption may promise a reply, a DM, a template, a checklist or a document.
  Nobody watches the inbox. Save / share / opinion prompts only.
