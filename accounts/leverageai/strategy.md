# Content strategy — maintained by the generator

This file is the loop's long-term memory. Each generation run reads it, writes
posts informed by it, and rewrites it based on the latest performance brief.
Conclusions accumulate here instead of being re-derived from raw numbers.

**Edit it freely.** Anything written here steers the next batch, so it is the
place to intervene by hand if the loop drifts.

---

## Positioning (owner decision, 2026-08-04 — unchanged)

Audience: **people who run their own business** — solo consultants, freelancers,
small operators. Every post is framed around a task a self-employed person
actually has (proposals, follow-ups, invoicing, scope creep, bookkeeping, their
own marketing) and the time or money it wins back.

## Working hypotheses (still unvalidated)

- Niche framing (self-employed tasks) converts reach into follows better than
  generic knowledge-worker framing. **Not yet measurable:** the repositioning
  landed 2026-08-04 and no post-repositioning reach data has come back.
- Copy-pasteable prompts drive saves. **Cannot test:** the brief reports no save
  counts. post02 (a prompt post) is the reach leader, which is suggestive and
  nothing more.
- Concrete-pain hooks ("Your proposal took 3 hours") beat topic-label hooks
  ("email workflow") for reach. Consistent with post01/post03 vs post02, but the
  brief does not record hooks, so this stays inference.
- Being explicit about limitations builds trust and helps saves. Untested.
- **New this batch:** news-pegged workflows (a rule that just took effect, a
  feature that just shipped) pull more reach than evergreen ones. Posts 015,
  017, 018, 020 are news-pegged; 016, 019, 021 are evergreen. Compare the two
  groups in the next brief.
- **New this batch:** money-pain topics (late invoices, unpaid scope creep) beat
  process topics for engagement. Posts 019 and 021 test this.

## Confirmed — do more of this

- **Every post must end in something usable.** The three highest-reach posts all
  hand over a finished artefact (prompt, checklist of mistakes, verdict); the
  three lowest hand over a topic or an opinion. Strongest signal in the data so
  far, and now a rule: no post ships without a copyable block or an explicit
  verdict. All seven posts in this batch carry a copyable block.
- Reels for discovery (inherited from the 2026-08-04 brief: reels at
  144/76/72/27/6 vs carousels at 1–3). **Not re-tested this run** — this batch is
  carousel-only because that is what the generation tool produces. If carousel
  reach for 015–021 stays at 1–3, the ceiling is structural and the owner needs
  to intervene on format, not on copy. Flagging that explicitly rather than
  pretending copy changes can fix it.

## Disproven — stop doing this

- **Meta-commentary about productivity/AI culture.** post11-fake-productivity
  reached 0 with zero engagement. No more posts about how people misuse AI in
  the abstract; write the task instead.
- **Topic-label framing on the cover.** "Tool duel" and "email workflow" both
  reached 2. Name the outcome or the pain, never the category.
