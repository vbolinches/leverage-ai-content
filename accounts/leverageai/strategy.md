# Content strategy — maintained by the generator

This file is the loop's long-term memory. Each generation run reads it, writes
posts informed by it, and rewrites it based on the latest performance brief.
Conclusions accumulate here instead of being re-derived from raw numbers.

**Edit it freely.** Anything written here steers the next batch, so it is the
place to intervene by hand if the loop drifts.

---

## Positioning (owner decision, 2026-08-04 — unchanged)

Audience: **people who run their own business** — solo consultants, freelancers,
small operators. Every post is framed around a task a self-employed person
actually has (proposals, follow-ups, invoicing, scope creep, bookkeeping, their
own marketing) and the time or money it wins back.

## Working hypotheses (still unvalidated)

- Niche framing (self-employed tasks) converts reach into follows better than
  generic knowledge-worker framing. **Weak support only:** followers went 7 → 8
  while the two best-engaged posts were the most niche. Sample far too small.
- Copy-pasteable prompts drive saves. **Still cannot test:** no save counts in
  any brief so far. post16 (prompt slide, concrete question hook) leads on
  likes-per-reach, which speaks to engagement, not saves.
- Concrete-pain hooks beat topic-label hooks for reach. Consistent with the one
  hook the brief records ("Did you use AI on this?", the engagement leader), but
  hooks are still not recorded for the rest. Stays inference.
- Being explicit about limitations builds trust and helps saves. Untested; kept
  because it is also just honest. Every post in this batch names a limitation.
- News-pegged workflows pull more reach than evergreen ones. **Carried forward
  unchanged — untestable this run.** Needs reach for 015, 017–021.
- Money-pain topics (late invoices, unpaid scope creep, surprise billing) beat
  process topics for engagement. **Carried forward — untestable;** 019 and 021
  have not reported. Posts 023 and 025 in this batch also test it.

## Confirmed — do more of this

- **Every post must end in something usable.** The posts that travel hand over a
  finished artefact (a prompt, a checklist, a verdict); the ones that reached 0–2
  handed over a topic or an opinion. Rule: nothing ships without a copyable block
  or an explicit verdict. All seven posts in this batch carry a prompt slide plus
  a named limitation.
- **Reels are the only discovery surface. Now confirmed, not inherited.** This
  brief labels format: reels 73/35/27/6, carousels 2/0. The generation tool
  produces carousels, so *copy cannot fix this* — the owner has to publish these
  as reels (cover slide as the hook frame) or reach stays capped in the low
  single digits regardless of quality. Highest-leverage intervention available
  and it is outside the generator's control.
- **Cover must name a pain or a change, never a category.** The only recorded
  hook that produced engagement is a client's question ("Did you use AI on
  this?"). Category labels ("tool duel", "email workflow") reached 2.

## Disproven — stop doing this

- **Meta-commentary about productivity/AI culture.** post11-fake-productivity
  reached 0 with zero engagement. Write the task, not the discourse.
- **Topic-label framing on the cover.** "Tool duel" and "email workflow" both
  reached 2.
- **Broad "here are some free tools" round-ups.** post07-free-tools-honest reached
  2 with the honesty angle intact, so honesty is not the problem — a round-up with
  no single decision to take away is. One task, one answer.
