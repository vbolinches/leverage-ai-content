# Content strategy — maintained by the generator

This file is the loop's long-term memory. Each generation run reads it, writes
posts informed by it, and rewrites it based on the latest performance brief.
Conclusions accumulate here instead of being re-derived from raw numbers.

**Edit it freely.** Anything written here steers the next batch, so it is the
place to intervene by hand if the loop drifts.

---

## Repositioning — 2026-08-04 (owner decision)

The account now targets **people who run their own business** — solo
consultants, freelancers, small operators. Not "knowledge workers" in general.
Every post should be framed around a task a self-employed person actually has
(client proposals, follow-ups, invoicing, scope creep, their own marketing,
bookkeeping) and the time or money it wins back. The generic-AI-tips space is
saturated; the niche is the reason to follow.

## Current read (2026-08-04, first data-backed brief)

10 posts, 7 followers (+6 in 10 days, flat since 08-01), ~330 total reach.

**Confirmed by data: Reels are the only discovery surface.** Reels reached
144 / 76 / 72 / 27 / 6; carousels reached 1–3. This is a 30–100x gap and it is
why the account went reel-heavy (2 reels per carousel) on 2026-08-04.

**The open problem is conversion, not reach.** ~350 strangers reached →
~5 follows, zero saves. Hypothesis: generic positioning gave no reason to
follow; the niche repositioning above is the countermeasure. Every reel now
ends with an explicit follow end-card.

**Reel variance is unexplained.** 144 vs 6 between reels of the same format.
Study what the brief says the top reels did. Write cover hooks that name a
concrete pain ("Your proposal took 3 hours. This takes 20 minutes.") rather
than a topic label.

## Working hypotheses (unvalidated)

- Niche framing (self-employed tasks) converts reach into follows better than
  generic knowledge-worker framing. **Measure: followers-per-reach after this
  batch vs before.**
- Concrete-pain hooks on reel covers beat topic-label hooks for reach.
- Copy-pasteable prompts are the differentiator. Posts with a `prompt` slide
  should outperform pure-advice posts.
- Naming real tools (ChatGPT, Claude, NotebookLM, Perplexity, Canva) beats
  generic "AI" framing.
- Honesty about limitations builds trust and may help saves.

## Confirmed — do more of this

- Reels for discovery. Carousels only serve existing followers; keep them for
  save-worthy reference content, at most 1 per 3 posts (2026-08-04, reach data
  across 10 posts).
